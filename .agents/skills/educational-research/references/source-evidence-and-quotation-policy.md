# Verification and public reproduction are different

Read [the v0.4.3 source policy](verbatim-and-abstract-policy.md). Privately verifying 100 actual author words gives no right to reproduce those words publicly. The conservative per-work public budget normally remains <=20 verbatim words, including repeats throughout the reader. Only exact matched original text may appear in quotes; explanation and our inference have distinct typed labels/counts.

For abstract-only works, public quotation requires a separately recorded permission/licence review. Otherwise use a faithful clearly labelled explanation and source link. Never reproduce an entire protected abstract by default. Verified CC BY or explicit permission can justify an increased documented allowance; a URL alone is not independent licence verification. Private exact spans, paper copies and real observations never enter public examples.
