# Five native gates and the scientific boundary

Complete gate/QA records remain private under evidence/. PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT_APPLICABLE are evidence states, not aesthetic grades. Overall research PASS requires every applicable hard gate and a non-synthetic substantive run; no automatic publishability or journal endorsement. `audit.passed` remains deprecated layout compatibility; use research_passed/overall_status. Strict CLI saves useful diagnostics and exits 3 for incomplete acceptance; structural validation can still recognise an honest provisional pack.

G1 requires real recorded ERIC responses and dated lawful Scholar observations/exports, original prospective protocol, actual query/filter/result IDs and observed artifact digests. Plans, cached metadata, retrospective invented logs and OpenAlex/Crossref/Unpaywall/CORE pointers do not substitute for an executed Scholar search. Missing/blocked providers remain explicit. Candidate breadth is logged/bounded, not a completeness guarantee. Acquisition separately records retrieval, content/identity/completeness, legal processing and version; downloads never satisfy reading by themselves.

G2 requires max(10,effective N,any higher explicit reading threshold) distinct, relevant, independently identified deep readings. Each non-synthetic roadmap work needs a validated native acquisition record plus actual lawful versioned/hash-checked artifact, permission evidence, reader/date/whole-work or justified theoretical-chapter scope, four independent checked source roles and >=100 useful English words of anchored explanation per work in the actual PDF. Exclude title/metadata/citations/locators/quotes and own inference, and reject duplicate boilerplate or unsupported prose. Exact source spans/context/warrant/qualification/epistemic kind must match the attached version. Short verified explanations stay provisional; no averaging/padding. Dynamic quotas remain intact: twenty requested and ten full texts plus ten abstracts is a ten-work reading shortfall. Changed versions, failed extraction or unclear processing rights prevent silent promotion.

G3 requires 3–5 main reader comparisons, two independent qualified readings each, their attributed positions/different checked passages, comparison, strength, warrant, actual counterargument/qualification, separate own inference and RQ/theory/paragraph links. Preserve declared negative/null evidence. No fabricated conflict or author-name collage. Full details remain in the private graph; reader prose is concise. Legacy evidence-pack may retain the older 3–7-claim allowance. Connected, explanatory framework and conditional method-fit must pass; software cannot certify semantic correctness.

G4 challenges novelty against 5–10 closest works where available, especially the strongest gap-destroying study, using original constructs/instruments/outcomes/context/method/findings and passage evidence. Verdicts: supported; narrow but defensible; refuted; insufficient primary evidence. A refuted gap is not retained as an asserted contribution: revise the question or report no supported novelty. A university/country/terminology/interview add-on alone is not a scholarly gap. Keep author-claimed gaps, researcher inference and empirical checks distinct and bounded to the actual search.

G5 validates BOTH products: all 15 private evidence dimensions/aliases and acquisition/handoff, reading/argument/novelty/framework/alignment/APA/protocol/gate/QA artifacts; plus the native five-section reader, one intact source block/reference per selected work, source-specific explanation wording/counts and cumulative quote budgets, consistent K/N and qualified counts, valid links/fonts/bounds/receipts. Actual visual review complements all-page automated checks. A custom PDF or reduced CSV that bypasses the native architecture fails.

Editorial assessment, when used, needs traceable rationale and judgement calls; source accuracy, novelty, theory/method fit cannot be rescued by a high cosmetic score. Scores and full audit matrices are private. Readiness means ready for expert review, never guaranteed acceptance. An accurate refuted gap or genuine access shortfall is a useful result; synthetic fixtures remain TEST ONLY/PARTIAL.
