# Five-section reader and complete private evidence

Default delivery: one `Research_Roadmap.pdf` and a one-paragraph note. The root has that PDF plus a concise README; complete editable provenance/audits remain private in evidence/. No second audit PDF by default. `--presentation evidence-pack` explicitly retains the legacy detailed layout; `--include-audit-pdf` explicitly adds that report under evidence/. Native CLI/renderer/G5 remain mandatory, not a detached attractive replacement.

The reader has exactly five sections, in order:

1. RESEARCH LOGIC: focused problem/RQ, provisional contribution and 3–4-step argument route, preferably within one page.
2. WHAT THE AUTHORS ACTUALLY ESTABLISH: every selected source appears once in an unboxed block, grouped by debate. Three analytical parts connect original position+method/reasoning, evidence+boundary, and brief our use. The first two provide >=100 English words of checked article-specific explanation per qualifying work, usually 100–140, excluding metadata/locators/quotes/own inference. Embed 1–2 authentic short fragments only where permitted. Short verified content stays provisional rather than padded.
3. ARGUMENT INTEGRATION + CLOSEST-STUDY CHALLENGE: 3–5 connected comparisons with real counterarguments and separate qualified synthesis; strongest rival, precise surviving difference or refutation, and bounded conclusion.
4. THEORETICAL / CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK + ROADMAP: selected parsimonious lens with brief reason, one readable connected diagram, exact construct distinctions and concise writing/research sequence. Include one essential feasibility gate/fallback when empirical.
5. APA 7 REFERENCES: all selected verified works, complete DOI/links and legible continuation pages.

A factual coverage/PROVISIONAL note is allowed. Never print search/HTTP error logs, hashes, schema fields, word counters, metric scores, gate statuses, candidates, giant matrices, textbook methods essays or generic introductions. Remove duplication, not source qualifications, counterevidence or locators. Selected works, qualified readings and excerpts have separate counts. Requested N governs strict deep readings max(10,N); default ten is a floor, not a cap. Exact/at_least and source/reading/evidence shortfalls must remain consistent across private records and the factual reader disclosure.

Freeze approved geometry: A4 portrait; white/black with fine grey rules; installed Times/Liberation Serif; title 18 pt, section 12 pt, body 10.5/14 pt, locators >=9.5 pt, running header 9 pt, centred folio 8 pt; exact outer horizontal bounds 56.7–538.6 pt, header rule 42.5 pt from top, footer 27 pt from bottom. No coloured panels, cards, rating widgets or decorative graphics. Keep each paper's core block together; balance reference continuations and keep headings with substantive following text. Pagination adapts to evidence; never shrink readable type or impose a fixed page count.

Private evidence retains all 15 original matrix dimensions and aliases, acquisition/status/handoff, local legal/version/artifact records, primary-word/quotation accounting, full reading ledger, argument graph, nearest-work challenge, rejected/blocked works, candidate theories, full construct/method alignment, CSL/APA checks, prospective protocol/discovery observations, G1–G5, QA, native receipts and actual visual-review attestations. None is replaced by the simplified PDF. Validate every page's fonts/bounds/glyphs/links/source/reference coverage and actually view first/evidence-dense/framework/middle/final images; code and appearance do not establish scientific validity.
