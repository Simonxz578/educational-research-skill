# Primary-text anchors and reader eligibility

Legacy v1/v2 inputs remain readable; old access labels never become genuine close reading automatically. The default roadmap promotes only highly relevant, independently identified works with an actual lawful versioned full-text file, SHA256, acquisition/read timestamps, reader and whole-work/relevant-chapter coverage. Abstracts and snippets remain follow-up candidates.

For v3 each selected work needs at least four independent meaningful checked anchors: thesis/concept definition; method/theoretical mechanism; result/developed reasoning; limitation/boundary/counterpoint. Empirical articles normally have 4–6. Theoretical work uses conceptual sections rather than invented samples/results. ReadingEvidence includes evidence_id, content_type, exact_span, locator, context, paraphrase, author_warrant, researcher_inference, status and finding_kind. Exact spans are private, literal source material; they must occur in the associated segment and actual hashed document. Repeated sentences cannot stand in for four roles. Unchanged source text cannot masquerade as a paraphrase.

Paraphrases must explain the author's actual meaning and warrant in context. Keep observed/tested findings, participant-reported accounts, interpretation and future potential separate. Every reader statement resolves to a source anchor/locator. The native Reader_View derives author position, two close reasoning/method/result paraphrases, limitations and our inference; no generic abstract summary substitutes. Contextual/interpretative accuracy remains the agent's scholarly responsibility, not mathematical certification by Python.

Record quote_policy: short_excerpts_permitted / no_reproduction / not_checked. Where quotable, choose 1–2 actual minimal fragments linked to substantive anchors, with precise known locators; a long original sentence is not a reason to exceed the copyright budget or invent wording. No-reproduction rights mean paraphrases and honest disclosure, not invented quotations. Quote/anchor/digest/locator uncertainty prevents promotion until repaired. Full text/segments and private anchors are not published in the repository.
