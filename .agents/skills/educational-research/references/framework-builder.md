# Explanatory framework with a connected small reader footprint

Use Framework in the native synthesis schema; the agent performs the literature-led reasoning and Python checks/renders explicit records. Extract the phenomenon, outcomes, mechanism and boundaries from actual primary-text readings. [Jabareen (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1177/160940690900800406) is a conceptual-framework method reference, not a guarantee that any assembled diagram is a validated predictive model. Never manufacture theory from topic keywords.

Compare 2–3 plausible lenses privately on explanation, epistemology, scope, checked evidence, operationalisation and method fit. Choose the parsimonious primary lens, or justify a necessary combination/no-single-theory synthesis. Record alternatives and rejection reasons in evidence/. Reader section IV shows only the selected lens with 2–4 lines of justification, one connected monochrome vector diagram, exact construct distinctions, key observables/analysis and a concise research/writing sequence. No decorative theory list or giant alignment/method table.

Every node needs a non-circular definition, supporting/contradicting anchors, source/claim/RQ links, proposed variable/interview code/document indicator, analysis use and disconfirming pattern. Every link needs its actual evidence, mechanism/conditions, rival mechanism and negative case; no isolated capability node or unexplained arrow. Source-grounded, proposed/hypothesised and sensitising links remain distinct. Directional arrows follow declared analytical mechanisms, not an inference of causality from association. Source-verified causal links require a defensible identification argument.

Check both directions through title/problem/gap/original author claims/theory/RQ/measure or code/analysis/interpretation/contribution. Preserve explicit evidence-led bridges for changed labels; do not replace aspirations with goal clarity, available resources with capability, participation with willingness, or non-use with exclusion. These are general construct-boundary examples rather than a hard-coded student/topic preset. Preserve external support, choice and informed non-use where supported. The framework must explain and constrain design, not decorate it.

State what the lens cannot show, the modest possible contribution, scope limits and what evidence would revise it. Unsupported, drifting or disconnected models are flagged privately and withheld from authoritative reader display. Native outputs retain full framework/diagram JSON, editable Mermaid/SVG, construct alignment and audit records; reader simplification never removes these. Actual theoretical adequacy and interpretation remain scholarly judgements.

If empirical, use [design feasibility](study-design-feasibility.md). Show one essential go/no-go condition and an honest fallback, with no invented access, approvals, participant numbers or programme data. An exploratory capability sequence is not automatically a causally identified regression or feasible mixed-methods study.
