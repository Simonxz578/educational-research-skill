# Metrics and selection

DORA https://sfdora.org/read/ advises assessing the work on its own merits. Clarivate JCR https://journalcitationreports.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/28351291240209-JCR-Best-Practices supplies journal context, not proof about individual articles.

Weights: relevance 45, study/evidence quality 20, journal influence 15, article-specific impact 10, argumentative contribution 10. Scores are assessed 0–100 then converted into weighted points; every assessment requires rationale/date. Relevance can be provisionally lexical, which must be disclosed. The CLI does not invent method scores from an abstract or convert raw citations into quality.

Study quality assesses actual design, sampling, transparency, analysis, comparisons and inference limits. Evaluate theoretical papers for conceptual coherence, explanatory power and engagement with rival accounts, not experimental-design criteria.

JIF must include licensed/official provenance, metric year and subject category; quartile requires provenance. Otherwise JIF: NOT VERIFIED. SJR, CiteScore and h5-index keep their own names, source, year, category. They are not interchangeable. Books/conferences without metrics are not excluded.

Article citations require named index and checked date; FWCI requires an actual provider value. No individual article impact factor. Recent articles and contrary/null work must not be punished for limited citation accumulation. Missing scores remain null; rescale available weights and show denominator/missingness. A provisional 90/100 based on lexical relevance alone is not comparable to a fully assessed score without inspecting the denominator.

Protect foundations, critical/null results and method challenges. Low relevance cannot be rescued by prestige; metrics never act as a hard exclusion gate. Rank is a decision aid; organise the review by debate/mechanism. Upload verified metrics in private source JSON; the package has no paywalled JCR connector.
