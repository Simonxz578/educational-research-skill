# Five-section reader projection

Reader_View.json exposes exactly the established five reader sections. ReaderWork.evidence_status is FULL_TEXT or ABSTRACT_ONLY; the private layers explicitly distinguish VERBATIM_PRIMARY_TEXT, SOURCE_ANCHORED_EXPLANATION and OUR_INTERPRETATION. verified_original_words describes actual full-text inspection; explanation length is diagnostic, not a second gate. Abstract source words have scope ABSTRACT_ONLY and confer zero full-text credit.

See [the authoritative policy](verbatim-and-abstract-policy.md). Source blocks show status, a permitted short original fragment where available, concise located explanation, a distinct use/boundary and a source link. Native G5 validates source wording, included references, scope/count agreement and receipts. Full private ledgers stay in evidence/.
