# Original-text reading, anchors and evidence depth

Read [lawful access](full-text-access.md) before acquisition. Legacy source inputs remain loadable, but old access labels and a successful download never establish genuine reading or current v0.4.2 eligibility. For non-synthetic default-roadmap sources a validated native acquisition record is required; manually setting a legacy full-text access label cannot substitute. Explicit lawful local user locations can be validated without network when their permitted scope is documented. The legacy evidence-pack remains the compatibility path for old input contracts. Read the actual permitted complete work or a justified relevant theoretical chapter; abstracts/snippets remain follow-up candidates. Record its version, local artifact/SHA256, acquisition/read timestamps, reader, scope, legal basis, processing permission and supporting evidence. Revalidation must resolve changed artifacts or version mismatches before old readings qualify.

Each work needs at least four independently meaningful checked anchors: thesis/construct definition; context+method or theoretical mechanism; precise result/developed reasoning; limitation/counterevidence. Empirical works normally support 4–6; theoretical chapters use actual conceptual reasoning rather than invented samples/results. ReadingEvidence retains evidence_id/content_type, exact private span, precise locator, contextual paraphrase, attributed author warrant, finding_kind, disconfirming qualification and separately labelled researcher_inference. Spans must occur in the associated segment and actual hashed artifact; an attached page number cannot authenticate invented words. Preserve HTML section/paragraph, JATS and PDF-version-specific locators without guessing printed pages.

The reader displays three connected analytical parts: original position+method/reasoning; finding/evidence+limitation; brief our use. The first two must independently provide >=100 English words per work of useful, source-specific, full-text-anchored explanation, usually 100–140. Count the four rendered checked paraphrases only. Exclude title, bibliography, citations/locators, quotes, generic context and our inference; never average across works. Repetition, boilerplate, source/paraphrase mismatch and unsupported fluent text cannot satisfy this requirement. Exact private spans are not automatically public prose.

If only 75 sound words are available, preserve those 75 instead of padding. An otherwise verified source can retain a visibly provisional explanation, but its evidence shortfall stays in the private audit and cannot count as a qualified deep reading for strict G2. Source selection, substantive qualification and public quotation counts remain separate. Lexical checks can flag problems; source-specific meaning, epistemic fidelity and comprehensive reading remain actual agent/human responsibilities.

Keep measured/tested outcomes, participant reports, theoretical argument, interpretation and future potential distinct. An association is not a causal effect, a reported experience is not prevalence, and intention is not observed participation. Each author assertion and limitation points to the exact checked evidence; own use is an explicitly separate inference.

Record quote_policy: short_excerpts_permitted / no_reproduction / not_checked. When permitted, embed one or two authentic short fragments linked to substantive anchors, with source-language wording and precise locators. Follow [quotation policy](source-evidence-and-quotation-policy.md). Never fabricate wording to meet a quota, redistribute private source text, or treat a successful machine extraction as comprehension.
