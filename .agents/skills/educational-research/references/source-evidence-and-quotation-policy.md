# v2 evidence policy

Source-author claim, source evidence and target-author inference remain distinct. Exact source spans require known locators and lawful source version. `Source.reading` is separate from old access flags: a DOI or an ERIC abstract does not establish full-text reading. Use the first-class reading audit and the close-reading reference.

Default v2 quote budget is 20 words per work; count CJK characters conservatively. Legacy v1 25-word records load without silent promotion but need reduction or legitimate rights provenance for strict v2 acceptance. A claimed full text needs an accessible hashed local document; checked passages and quotes are tested against its native text. These checks cannot certify external authenticity or interpretative accuracy. OCR/scanned material requires explicit additional verification.

Never convert participant confidence/aspirations/intention into observed behaviour, correlation into causality, a proposed mechanism into a finding, or an author normative hope into tested effectiveness. Preserve method and limitations as part of the warrant. Keep protected source bodies in private inputs; outputs retain locators/paraphrases rather than whole source documents. Never commit user manuscripts, real student records, paid PDFs or observation artifacts to the public repository.
