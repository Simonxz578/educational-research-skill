# Education-first protocol

Before selection record date, exact topic/RQ, concepts/mechanisms, learners/teachers/schools, educational age/stage, geography, period, outcomes, design, theoretical traditions, language and eligibility rules. Treat unspecified dimensions as provisional, not facts about the user.

Offer up to three questions: conceptual (construct and operationalisation), explanatory (mechanism and context) and comparative (rival explanations or method differences). Explain assumptions, available evidence and testability; select the most defensible provisional question. Do not require a questionnaire before useful discovery.

Separate practical problem, scholarly puzzle, empirical absence, conceptual tension, theory assumption and possible contribution. Theory must do analytical work: define constructs, expected relationships, observable indicators, alternative explanations and limits. Remove obsolete questions/theory when scope changes; retain a trace to the affected claim.

Preserve prospective protocol plus amendments, exact searches, counts, dedup decisions, screening reasons and checked dates. Select for content and methodological fit before influence. Expand a small pool with adjacent terms, British/American spellings, null findings, reviews, foundational theory and recent evidence. A systematic-review claim requires actual systematic methods; PRISMA-inspired logging alone is insufficient.
