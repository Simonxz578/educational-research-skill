# Reader output contract

Keep the native Research_Roadmap.pdf plus one short delivery note; technical records stay in private evidence/. Exactly five sections: research logic; authors' evidence; integration/closest-study challenge; framework/roadmap; APA references. Preserve approved A4 monochrome Times/Liberation Serif geometry, thin rules, adaptive readable pagination and open source blocks. No colour cards, audit tables or reduced type.

Every included work appears with FULL TEXT or ABSTRACT ONLY status and a complete reference. Coverage separates included N, verified full texts K, abstracts A and unfilled target items. A provisional abstract-rich reader is useful even when strict G2 fails. Label source-informed prose as explanation/paraphrase, and only matched original text as a quotation. Do not print the private 100-word inspection ledger or imply a 100-word AI-writing quota. See [the v0.4.3 policy](verbatim-and-abstract-policy.md).
