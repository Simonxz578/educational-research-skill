# Source claim → evidence → target argument

For each source preserve objective, theory, design/sample/context, evidence, author's actual argument, limitations, relevance, target author's independent argument and cross-paper tensions. Every substantive extracted summary/argument links to local evidence segment IDs and records basis (`source-author claim`, `source evidence`, `researcher interpretation`). These links support review, not automatic proof of interpretation.

Access: FULL_TEXT_VERIFIED, ABSTRACT_ONLY, METADATA_ONLY, USER_SUPPLIED, UNAVAILABLE. A lawful user upload is not independently authenticated. Full text uses native PDF extraction first; OCR must be explicit and verified. PDF page and printed page differ; never invent the latter. Preserve section/paragraph locators and source URL, access date and licence.

Two or three tiny source-language quotes are useful when available. Default combined budget is 25 whitespace-delimited words per source; for CJK also conservatively count characters rather than treating an entire sentence as one word. A larger budget requires documented permissive rights and proportional use. Quotations must match an exact segment span and carry known locator and access provenance. Abstract/metadata-only records show NO VERIFIED QUOTE. Translations are labelled user or published and never passed off as literal original text. Licence provenance and external authenticity still require human/agent checking.

Self-report measures reported experience; retrospective testimony is not observation; association is not causation; proposed methods are not completed studies. Student outcomes, teacher perceptions, school-level processes and theoretical propositions require different inference boundaries. Flag unsupported causal language and future research disguised as findings. Respect paywalls, subscriptions and CAPTCHA. Do not bundle protected full texts, source uploads or private feedback into public outputs/repository.
