# Fewer comparative claims and a falsifiable novelty challenge

Read the closest substantive works before promoting novelty. Compare 5–10 genuinely relevant works where available: exact RQ, population/stage, institutional/geographic context and timing, constructs/instruments/outcomes, design/sample, theory, findings, limitations, overlap and substantive remainder. Each comparison needs checked primary-text evidence. If access or coverage is incomplete, report a bounded shortfall rather than pad with abstract-level claims.

Identify the strongest potentially gap-destroying study, not only agreeable predecessors. Ask whether its original measures/results already answer the proposed question. State a tentative falsifiable contribution; argue against it before defending any precise surviving difference. Return supported, narrow but defensible, refuted, or insufficient primary evidence. Geography/university/population/terminology substitutions or adding interviews alone do not establish novelty. When refuted, withhold the unsupported selling point, revise the question if evidence warrants one, or explicitly conclude no supported gap. Never invent a revision as though tested or pre-commit to GAP_SUPPORTED.

Build 3–5 decisive connected reader chains. Each needs two independent qualified full-text readings with different traceable anchors, actual author positions and methods/findings, comparison, relative strength, warrant, counterargument, qualified own inference and RQ/framework/paragraph destination. Preserve actual negative/null results or report their absence honestly; do not fabricate disagreement or turn conflicting evidence into consensus. Reader prose is 2–4 lucid sentences per chain; the private graph retains full warrant/objection IDs, scope, evidence links and paragraph plan. Legacy evidence-pack may retain its older 3–7-claim contract.

Graph relations include supports, contradicts, qualifies, extends, different_context, method_incompatible, same_construct_different_measure and dependencies. Content relations resolve to actual source passages, not author names alone. Qualitative reports, correlations, tested effects, theoretical arguments and future potential remain epistemically distinct. A source-rich paragraph roadmap explains what the next paragraph argues, which authors support/qualify it, the warrant and why the next step follows; do not generate a long ghostwritten review by default.

`Argument_Alignment.json` preserves title/problem/RQ ↔ source claims ↔ gap ↔ theory/construct definitions ↔ measure/code ↔ analysis ↔ interpretation/contribution, with reverse stage/construct indexing. Flag unbridged construct substitutions where they occur. Framework/design/semantic adequacy remains actual scholarly work; a source ID, hash or passing graph cannot certify comprehension or publishability.
